About Golden-Egg

The Project

Golden-Egg is a digital art collection created to explore the combination of digital artwork, decentralized storage, smart contracts and blockchain-based ownership.

The artwork and metadata references use IPFS, while the ERC-721 tokens are recorded on the Ethereum Sepolia Testnet.

This website is a portfolio and technical showcase of the project.
