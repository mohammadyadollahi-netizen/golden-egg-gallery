✦ About The Project

Golden-Egg

Golden-Egg is a digital art project exploring the connection between visual creativity, decentralized storage and blockchain-based digital ownership.

ERC-721 GEGG IPFS Ethereum Sepolia

Project Vision

The idea behind Golden-Egg.

Digital Art With Provenance

Golden-Egg combines digital artwork with blockchain records so that each minted artwork can be identified by its collection contract and Token ID.

Built To Grow

The project is structured as a collection that can evolve over time with additional artworks, improved presentation and future development.

Technology

The core technologies used by the project.

⛓️

Ethereum

Blockchain infrastructure for the ERC-721 contract and NFT ownership records.

🎨

ERC-721

The NFT standard used by the Golden-Egg collection contract.

📦

Content-addressed storage for artwork files and NFT metadata.
