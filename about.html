About the Project

Golden-Egg is an experimental digital art collection created to explore the foundations of NFT technology and blockchain-based digital ownership.

Each artwork in the collection is represented by an ERC-721 token on the Ethereum Sepolia Testnet. The collection currently contains four minted tokens, from Golden-Egg #1 through Golden-Egg #4.

The project combines digital artwork, blockchain records, NFT metadata and IPFS content-addressed storage into a single verifiable structure.

IPFS & Metadata

The Golden-Egg artworks and their NFT metadata are referenced using IPFS content identifiers (CIDs).

Each token has an individual image CID and metadata CID. These identifiers allow the associated digital content to be independently referenced and verified.
