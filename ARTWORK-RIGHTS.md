## Copyright and Usage

The Golden-Egg artworks, including:

- `golden-egg-1.png`
- `golden-egg-2.png`
- Future Golden-Egg collection artworks
- Collection artwork and visual assets
- Golden-Egg branding and visual identity

are creative assets of the Golden-Egg Project.

Unless explicit permission is granted, no license is provided for:

- commercial reproduction
- redistribution
- modification or derivative artwork
- resale of the artwork files
- use in another NFT collection
- use as a commercial brand or product identity

## Permitted Repository Viewing

The images may be viewed through the GitHub repository and the official
Golden-Egg Gallery for documentation, portfolio, and collection-reference
purposes.

## NFT Ownership

Ownership of an NFT token does not automatically grant copyright ownership
of the underlying artwork.

The blockchain token represents the NFT according to the smart contract and
associated metadata. Intellectual-property rights in the artwork are
separate from token ownership unless a separate written license states
otherwise.
