The artwork files may be viewed through the official Golden-Egg repository and public gallery for purposes such as:

## NFT Ownership

Ownership of an NFT token does not automatically transfer copyright ownership of the underlying artwork.

The blockchain token represents the NFT according to the deployed ERC-721 smart contract and its associated metadata.

Intellectual-property rights in the artwork are separate from NFT token ownership unless a separate written license explicitly states otherwise.
